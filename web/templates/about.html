From Citicorp High Finance to Grassroots Transformation

Mr. Tushar Dodia represents a rare convergence in Indian education and corporate leadership: an elite quantitative finance technocrat who deeply reveres classical Indian philosophy and civilizational ethos.

After earning a Gold Medal in Computer Engineering and completing his MBA in Finance with a prestigious CFA Scholarship from Mumbai University, he achieved dual global professional benchmarks: the CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst, USA) and FRM (Financial Risk Manager, USA). He spent 8 years commanding software development and financial analytics at Persistent Systems Pune, Fidelity Information Services (FIS) Mumbai, and as a Finance Manager at Citicorp.
